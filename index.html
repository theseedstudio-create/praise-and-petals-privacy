Privacy Policy for Praise & Petals

Last Updated: May 20, 2026

Melanie Mejia ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Praise & Petals mobile application (the "Service"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information and spiritual reflections you entrust to our app.

1. Information Collection and Use

Praise & Petals is designed as a private, local-first journaling application.

  • Journal Entries & Data: All journal entries, prayers, sticker selections, and app data are stored directly on your physical device using secure local storage. We do not transmit, upload, or store your journal entries on external servers.
  • Personal Data: We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use the core features of the application.

2. Third-Party Services

The Service does not use third-party analytics, tracking tools, or advertising networks that collect or share your data.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data is stored entirely on your device, we have no access to it. Your data is retained on your device for as long as the app is installed. If you wish to delete your data, you can do so by clearing the app data through your device settings or by uninstalling the application. Note: Deleting the app will permanently delete your journal entries, as we do not keep a backup cloud copy.

4. Security

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We rely on the native security features of Apple's iOS to keep your local app data safe. We encourage you to protect your device with a passcode, Touch ID, or Face ID.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.
